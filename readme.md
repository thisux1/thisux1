## About me

I'm Thiago — a software engineer, builder, and compulsive rabbit-hole explorer from São Paulo.

I build things because I want to understand how they work. Most projects start with a simple *"what if?"* and somehow end with me touching the database, API, interface, deployment — and occasionally a microcontroller.

I enjoy working across boundaries: **product and engineering, software and hardware, deterministic systems and AI**. I'm especially interested in agentic systems, automation, ML, distributed workflows, and interfaces that make complex systems feel simple.

I care about the whole thing, not just the code. Architecture, behavior, performance, UX, failure modes, and the tiny details that make something feel intentional.

Outside software, I tend to disappear into rabbit holes about **physics, cosmology, psychology, philosophy, photography, and music**.

> I like building systems that do something real.
