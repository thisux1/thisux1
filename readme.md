## About me

I'm Thiago — a software engineer, builder, and compulsive rabbit-hole explorer from São Paulo.

I build things because I want to understand how they work. Most projects start with a simple *"what if?"* and somehow end with me touching the database, API, interface, deployment — and occasionally a microcontroller.

I enjoy working across boundaries: **product and engineering, software and hardware, deterministic systems and AI**. I'm especially interested in agentic systems, automation, ML, distributed workflows, and interfaces that make complex systems feel simple.

I care about the whole thing, not just the code. Architecture, behavior, performance, UX, failure modes, and the tiny details that make something feel intentional.

Outside software, I tend to disappear into rabbit holes about **physics, cosmology, psychology, philosophy, photography, and music**.

> I like building systems that do something real.

**Currently exploring:** agentic infrastructure · applied AI · automation · embedded systems

**Usually working with:** TypeScript · Python · React · Node.js · PostgreSQL · ESP32

**Based in:** São Paulo, Brazil 🇧🇷
**Languages:** Portuguese · English
**Website:** [thisux.tech](https://thisux.tech)

## Selected work

### `agentpay` — agentic payments infrastructure

Authorization infrastructure for purchases made by AI agents.

Deterministic purchase mandates, real-time policy validation and auditable transaction flows designed around a buyer that isn't human.

**MCP · TypeScript SDK · REST APIs · payment infrastructure**

Winner of the **NextWave Hackathon 2026 — LATAM**.

→ [Project](https://nextwave-hackathon-2026.vercel.app/t/H3MDSM)

---

### `correio elegante` — payments & product

A digital love-letter platform built as a complete product rather than a frontend demo.

Themed cards, media uploads, public sharing and payment-gated delivery with real payment flows.

**React 19 · Express 5 · Prisma · MongoDB · Stripe · PagBank / Pix**

---

### `singular` — document intelligence

A pipeline that turns documents into structured quizzes.

Instead of treating generation as a single HTTP request, ingestion runs as an asynchronous workflow with document parsing, OCR, AI generation and background jobs.

**Python · Hono · BullMQ · PostgreSQL · Drizzle · React PWA**

---

### `oracle` — automation & ML

A state-machine companion for EPIC RPG that automates gameplay while handling verification challenges locally.

Its CNN solves captcha images offline without relying on external vision APIs.

**Python · TensorFlow Lite · FastAPI · Textual · PyInstaller**

---

### `aeroglove` — embedded systems

A gesture-controlled interface for flying a custom drone.

An ESP32-S3 reads IMU data, performs sensor fusion at 100 Hz and translates hand orientation into wireless flight commands.

**ESP32-S3 · MicroPython · Madgwick · ESP-NOW · BLE · MPU9250**
